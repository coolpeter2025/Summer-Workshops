About the Workshop

My Purpose Kids Summer Workshop

Christ-Centered creative learning and fun activities for kids ages 6–12! Join us every Tuesday and Thursday from 9:30 AM to 1:00 PM in beautiful Tarpon Springs for faith-based education and fellowship.

We keep groups small on purpose — every child gets eye contact, their name remembered, and a leader invested in their growth.

Age Requirements

This workshop is designed for children ages 6–12.

What Your Kids Will Do

Four pillars, twelve mornings, one great summer.

Every session rotates through these four areas — so kids stay engaged, bodies get moving, and nobody sits at a table for three hours.

Faith & Learning

Short, age-appropriate Bible lessons led by trained leaders — stories kids can actually retell over dinner.

Creative Arts & Crafts

Hands-on projects kids take home — paint, paper, clay, and the occasional glitter explosion.

Teamwork & Games

Cooperative games and group challenges that build real friendships — the kind that last past July.

Active Play

Outdoor time, movement breaks, and plenty of running — kids arrive home tired in the best possible way.

A Typical Day

What a Tuesday or Thursday looks like.

Three and a half hours, four rotations, one snack break. Parents pick up at 1:00 PM sharp.

9:30 AM – 1:00 PM

Every Tuesday & Thursday

Drop-off opens at 9:15. Please bring a labeled lunchbox and a refillable water bottle — we don't sell snacks on-site.

9:30 AM
Welcome & Opening Circle
Name tags, worship song, prayer, theme intro
9:50 AM
Bible Story & Discussion
Age-grouped — a story + three questions
10:30 AM
Creative Craft Rotation
Take-home project tied to the day's theme
11:30 AM
Snack & Lunch
Kids eat what you packed — water refills on us
12:00 PM
Active Play & Games
Outdoor when weather permits, indoor if not
12:45 PM
Closing Circle & Pickup
Recap, prayer, handoff to parents at 1:00
